07 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

RentFolio questions, answered.

What is RentFolio?

RentFolio is a digital catalog and rental availability management system for bridal studios and clothing rental businesses. It helps businesses present their collections, track stock by size or variant, and receive booking inquiries.

How does RentFolio help prevent double-bookings?

RentFolio checks item stock and rental date overlaps so businesses can see availability for selected dates and variants before responding to an inquiry.

Can customers book and pay through a public checkout?

RentFolio is designed for catalog browsing and direct inquiries, not public checkout. Customers can send a structured booking request to the business through WhatsApp, where the team can follow up.

Who is RentFolio designed for?

RentFolio is suitable for bridal and wedding attire studios, event and costume rental businesses, fashion boutiques, designers, cosplay rentals, and studio wardrobes.
