Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about BillFolio

What is BillFolio?

BillFolio is web-based invoicing and cash flow software for freelancers, agencies, and growing businesses. It helps teams create invoices with AI assistance, manage clients, share invoice links, accept online payments, and track payment status.

Can BillFolio help draft invoices with AI?

Yes. Enter a short text prompt and BillFolio can help prepare invoice line items, prices, and due dates for review.

Which online payment methods are available?

BillFolio provides public payment links through Midtrans. Clients can pay online using supported methods such as QRIS, GoPay, and bank virtual accounts. Invoice payment status can update automatically through webhooks.

Can I manage multiple companies and team members?

Yes. One account can manage multiple workspaces or companies, and each company can add team members with defined access.

Who is BillFolio designed for?

BillFolio is designed for freelancers, agencies, small businesses, and teams that create client invoices and need a clearer way to organize payments.
